# Hi, I'm Devansh Palan

Final-year Computer Science student at VNIT Nagpur, most at home where backend engineering meets AI — designing systems, wiring up APIs, and making LLM-based tools reliable rather than just impressive in a demo.

I like building things that actually get used. That's taken me from training reinforcement learning agents in a robotics lab to shipping full-stack products and, most recently, working on internal platforms at NatWest Group.

I care about clean code, measurable outcomes, and understanding how all the pieces fit together. Outside coursework, I build side projects end to end, solve DSA problems regularly, and keep exploring what's possible with agentic AI.

Graduating in 2027 and open to SDE/SWE roles. Happy to connect — or take the interactive tour at [devanshpalan.vercel.app](https://devanshpalan.vercel.app).

## Projects

- [**RepoBot**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/RepoBot) — codebase Q&A and PR-review agent. Indexes a repository with tree-sitter and grounds every answer in retrieved code before responding.
- [**Automation**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/Automation) — Node.js + TypeScript service that automates two government self-service portals with Playwright. Dockerized, with Jest test coverage.
- [**HisaabPakka**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/HisaabPakka) — finance reconciliation agent where LLM suggestions are checked by deterministic arithmetic before anything is accepted.
- [**Hostel Grievance Management System**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/Hostel-Grievance-Management-System) — full-stack complaint portal for hostels. Gemini classifies complaint priority; email and SMS notifications keep everyone in the loop.
- [**ZapCompare**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/ZapCompare) — compares grocery prices across Blinkit, Swiggy Instamart and Zepto, with Redis caching and JWT auth.
- [**VoiceCloning**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/VoiceCloning) — three-stage neural voice cloning pipeline: speaker encoder, Tacotron synthesizer, WaveRNN vocoder.
- [**Deep RL @ IVLabs**](https://github.com/devansh-palan/Summer-Intern-at-IVLabs-2024-Deep-Reinforcement-Learning) — reinforcement learning from dynamic programming up to DQN and PPO, trained on FrozenLake, MiniGrid and ViZDoom.
